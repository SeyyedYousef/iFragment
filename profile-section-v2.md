## Quick Summary

9-section profile redesign leveraging ALL Telegram WebApp APIs:

1. **Identity Hero** — Animated avatar ring, level badge, copy actions
2. **FRG Wallet Card** — Glassmorphic balance with sparkline
3. **Stats Dashboard** — 7 activity metrics in 2-col grid
4. **Achievement Gallery** — 20 collectible badges across 6 categories
5. **Referral Hub** — Share, QR scan, inline query, story share
6. **Quick Actions** — Home screen, emoji status, share, support
7. **Settings** — Language, notifications, haptic, sounds
8. **Account & Security** — Biometric lock, export, delete
9. **About & Legal** — Version, privacy, terms

## Key Differentiators (vs Blum/Notcoin/Hamster)
- ✅ Biometric Lock (none of them have it)
- ✅ Emoji Status Sync (exclusive)
- ✅ QR Referral Scanner (rare)
- ✅ Achievement System with Story sharing
